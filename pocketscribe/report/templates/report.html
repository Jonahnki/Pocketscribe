What this report is

Pocketscribe took a structure predicted by {{ primary.source.display_name }}, carried its per-residue confidence through every stage, and annotated each detected cavity with what that confidence means for interpreting it. The deliverable is triage: a ranked, caveated shortlist to help decide where to spend docking, simulation or wet-lab effort — not a substitute for any of them.

Confidence thresholds

Residues are banded at pLDDT 90, 70 and 50, following the conventional AlphaFold interpretation. A contiguous run of {{ min_region_length }} or more residues below a threshold is reported as a low-confidence region. A pocket’s caveat level is escalated on either a low mean confidence across its lining residues or a substantial minority of untrustworthy residues, because a binding site needs only a few badly-placed lining residues to be geometrically wrong.

{% if primary_pockets.backend_is_fpocket %}

Reproducibility of these numbers

fpocket estimates pocket volume by Monte Carlo integration, so volumes are not exactly reproducible between runs: re-running this analysis on the same structure typically shifts each volume by around a percent. Druggability scores, the pocket ranking and the lining-residue lists are stable. If you are comparing two reports, a small difference in the volume column is expected and is not evidence that anything changed.

Cross-model consensus is unaffected: pockets are matched on lining-residue overlap and centroid distance, neither of which is stochastic.

{% endif %} {% if not primary_pockets.backend_is_fpocket %}

Built-in heuristic score (used in this run)

0.40 × volume_term + 0.30 × buriedness_term + 0.30 × apolar_term, where volume_term is a logistic in pocket volume centred on 300 Å³ with a 150 Å³ width, buriedness_term is the mean 7-direction enclosure count rescaled to 0–1, and apolar_term is the fraction of lining heavy atoms that are carbon, sulphur or selenium. The weights are a reasoned ordering, not a fit to any benchmark, and the score carries no validation. It exists so the pipeline can run without fpocket; it is not a druggability model.

{% endif %} {% if result.consensus %}

Cross-model consensus

Structures were required to share at least {{ '%.0f'|format(result.consensus.parameters.min_sequence_identity * 100) }}% sequence identity. Residues were put into correspondence by global pairwise alignment, and models were superposed onto the first by Kabsch superposition of corresponding Cα atoms. Two pockets from different models were treated as the same pocket only when both criteria held: lining-residue Jaccard overlap (mapped through the alignment) of at least {{ result.consensus.parameters.min_residue_jaccard }}, and post-superposition centroid separation of at most {{ result.consensus.parameters.centroid_tolerance }} Å.

Each source is tagged with an architecture family — MSA/co-evolution-based (AlphaFold2, OpenFold, ColabFold, Boltz) or single-sequence language model (ESMFold). For a matched group, every pair of detecting models is classified as cross-family or same-family. Agreement is the sum of pair weights divided by the number of pairs of that kind that the input set could have produced, so 1.0 means every possible agreement of that kind occurred. {% if result.consensus.parameters.confidence_weighting %} Pair weights are the product of the two models’ mean pLDDT over the pocket’s lining residues, each rescaled to 0–1, so a pocket predicted confidently by both models counts for more than one predicted marginally by both. {% else %} Confidence weighting was disabled for this run, so every agreeing pair counts equally. {% endif %} A source whose family is unknown never counts as cross-family.

{% endif %}

Scope and limitations

This analysis is for research use only. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic, and carries no clinical or therapeutic claim of any kind. Nothing in it should be used to support a statement about treating any disease in any patient.

This is a predicted structure, not an experimental one. Every pocket described here exists in a computational model. Predicted models can place side chains wrongly, close cavities that are open in reality, and open cavities that are not. Apo predictions in particular often miss the conformational change that accompanies ligand binding. The confidence caveats attached to each pocket are the first thing to read, not a footnote.

Pocket detection and druggability scoring are heuristics. A high score means a cavity has the geometry and chemistry that tend to accompany ligandable sites. It is not evidence that any compound binds, and it says nothing about selectivity, affinity or tractability.

No simulation was run. The MD section generates input files. Their parameters are defaults chosen to be reasonable, not a protocol validated for this system.

This tool is triage, not judgement. It exists to help a group decide where to spend expert time, docking campaigns, simulation and experiments — never to replace them, and never to replace a structural biologist reading the structure.
